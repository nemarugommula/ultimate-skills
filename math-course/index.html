🔬 How to study this course
  • Play first, read the symbols second. Push the sliders until you can predict what happens. Then the equation is just a description of what you already saw.
  • Do the worked example with a pen. Small numbers by hand beat reading ten pages.
  • Do every quiz. Getting one wrong is the useful part: read the explanation, then hit ↻ Try again. A few minutes on the review page every couple of days brings old questions back before you forget them.
  • Do the labs. Coding the math yourself in NumPy (one lab per phase, in labs/) is what makes it stick.
  • Stuck on a symbol? The glossary reads every symbol out loud in plain English and links to the lesson that teaches it.
  • Teach it back. At the end of each phase, explain the big idea out loud in 60 seconds.
  • Taking the RL course too? Each 🌉 bridge box links to the RL lesson that uses the idea. Use ← / → to move between lessons.
