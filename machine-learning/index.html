🔬 How to study this course
  • Guess before you click. Most playgrounds ask you to predict what will happen. Being wrong is where the learning is.
  • Break the playgrounds. Overfit the tree, leak the target, pick the wrong k. Then fix it.
  • Do the labs. Each phase has a Python lab in labs/ (scikit-learn on tiny datasets, seconds to run).
  • Answer “why this?” out loud. For every method: what problem it fixes, why it works, what it costs, when it fails. That is the interview.
  • Review a little every day. 🔁 Review brings old quizzes back just before you forget them. Use ← / → to move between lessons.
